## DEMO NARRATIVE (why this denial is appealable — for our own testing, not shown to the agent)
- The MRI **did** require prior authorization (Section 1.1 / 4) — so that part may be valid UNLESS it was emergency-related (Section 4 retrospective review).
- BUT if the imaging was ordered following an **ER visit** (emergency), Section 1.4 says emergency services need **no** prior auth and are paid at In-Network rate regardless of facility — making the Out-of-Network downgrade improper.
